# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

TikTok Shop sellers — two overlapping groups:

1. **Existing TikTok Shop sellers** who have seen the creator's videos and want to understand how they achieve the same production quality and style at the top of the platform.
2. **New creators** who have discovered the platform through the creator's success and want to replicate the results from scratch.

Context: these users actively message the creator on TikTok Shop asking to learn. The demand is real and specific. They are not casual readers — they have a concrete goal (build a top-performing TikTok Shop) and a specific frustration (not knowing the system behind it).

## Product Purpose

A platform where a top-performing TikTok Shop creator shares their exact video-creation system — guides, playbooks, and tools — with sellers who want the same results. Phase 1 is a knowledge and media product. Later phases expand to AI-powered tools and content generation.

The product exists because the demand already exists. People keep asking. This is the answer.

## Brand Personality

Earned authority. Peer-to-peer. Premium without pretension.

The voice is not a faceless brand — it's a creator speaking directly to other creators. The platform has credibility because the creator has done the thing they're teaching. The design should feel like a premium playbook, not a "guru's course" and not a corporate brand.

Three words: **proven, direct, premium**.

## Anti-references

- Side-hustle blog aesthetics: "Make $10k/month!", beach laptop stock photos, emoji-heavy copy, countdown timers, fake scarcity
- Generic SaaS landing page: floating UI screenshots, purple mesh gradients, "enterprise-grade" language
- Guru energy: testimonial walls, overpromising, "secret method" framing
- Corporate B2B: navy + white, ROI-speak, stock photo office workers

## Design Principles

1. **Outcome-first** — every headline names a specific result, not a feature or category
2. **Creator-to-creator** — the voice comes from someone who has done this; the design reflects that authority without performing it
3. **Premium signals trust** — design quality should match the quality of the advice; if it looks cheap, the advice feels cheap
4. **Show the gap** — the hero should make a visitor feel the distance between where they are and where they could be, not just describe what the product is
5. **Specific over vague** — "TikTok Shop video creation" beats "income streams"; specificity builds credibility and filters for the right audience

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Amber accent on dark background — verify contrast at all sizes. Body text at sufficient size (≥16px). No motion-dependent critical information.

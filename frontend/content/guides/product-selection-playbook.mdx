The free guide covered the first two filters. This is the complete system: all five filters, how to apply them in sequence, and worked examples from products I've actually filmed.

## Why Product Selection Determines Everything

A video can be perfectly produced, hooked, scripted, and edited : and still generate almost no sales if the product is wrong. The product's job is to convert the attention your video built. If the product can't do that job (wrong price, weak listing, no demand), no amount of production quality fixes it.

The five-filter system runs in sequence. A product that fails Filter 1 doesn't go to Filter 2. You're looking for products that pass all five. Most won't. That's correct : the system is supposed to be selective.

## Filter 1: Visual Payoff (Can It Be Shown?)

Already covered in the free guide, but here's the expanded version.

The question isn't just "can I show this product?" It's "can I show a compelling result from this product in under 10 seconds of video?"

Products that pass: anything with a visible transformation, a satisfying mechanism, or an impressive result that can be captured in a clip. Skincare with visible texture. Gadgets with a satisfying function. Food with a compelling preparation or result. Organizational products with a before/after state.

Products that fail: anything that works invisibly (supplements, most financial products), anything that requires explanation (complex tools, services), anything where the value is only apparent over time (most health products).

AI skeleton videos specifically need products that look good in motion. A product that photographs well but moves awkwardly on video is a Filter 1 fail for this format.

## Filter 2: Impulse Price Range

Also covered in the free guide. The expanded version:

The impulse threshold for TikTok Shop is approximately $15-$65 for most categories. Below $15, the commission doesn't justify the production cost. Above $65, decision friction increases significantly.

There are exceptions. Beauty products can convert at $80-$120 if the visual payoff is exceptional. Tech accessories can convert at $80-$150 if the status signal is clear. But these require much stronger production and much better hooks than the $15-$65 range.

Start in the $25-$55 sweet spot and move outside it only when you have performance data showing your audience converts there.

## Filter 3: Margin vs. Production Cost

Commission rates on TikTok Shop range from 5% to 25% depending on the product and your affiliate tier. Before filming, calculate:

**Expected commission per sale:** Product price × commission rate

**Cost of producing one video:** Your time at whatever you value it at, plus any tool costs (approximately $2-5 per video in AI generation credits at the volume in this guide)

**Break-even number of sales:** Production cost / expected commission per sale

If break-even is more than 10 sales, the math is hard. If break-even is 2-3 sales, the product has good margin for the production effort.

Example:
- $45 product, 12% commission = $5.40 per sale
- Video production cost = $10 (2-3 hours + generation credits)
- Break-even = 2 sales

That's a product worth filming. Compare:
- $18 product, 5% commission = $0.90 per sale
- Same production cost = $10
- Break-even = 12 sales

That product needs to generate significantly more volume to justify the same production effort.

## Filter 4: Demand Signal Strength

You want to promote products where demand already exists, not products you'd need to create demand for. TikTok is a discovery platform : it's excellent at surfacing things people didn't know they wanted. But "didn't know they wanted" is different from "genuinely don't want."

How to check demand:
1. Search the product category on TikTok. Are there existing videos with strong view counts (100K+)?
2. Check the product on Amazon. Is it ranking in its category? How many reviews?
3. Look at the TikTok Shop bestseller lists in the relevant category.

Strong demand signal: multiple existing videos with 200K+ views, product with 500+ reviews on Amazon, appearing in TikTok Shop bestseller lists.

Weak demand signal: few or no existing TikTok videos, product is new with under 50 reviews, not appearing in any curated lists.

You don't need all three signals. Two strong signals is sufficient. But zero or one signal means you're betting on demand that may not materialize.

## Filter 5: Competitive Saturation by Format

This is the filter that eliminates the most products that would otherwise look attractive.

A product can have strong demand and still be oversaturated for the AI skeleton format. If 20 other creators are already making AI skeleton videos for the same product, your video enters a crowded feed. You're competing for the same search queries, the same audience's attention, and often the same TikTok Shop listing review section.

How to check format saturation:
1. Search the product on TikTok
2. Filter results to "most recent"
3. Count how many of the recent videos are clearly AI-generated skeleton videos

If more than 5 recent videos are AI skeleton format, saturation is high. If fewer than 3, there's space.

Counter-intuitive insight: high saturation sometimes indicates high conversion, not just high competition. A saturated product is saturated because it's converting. The play in that case is to differentiate your hook and angle, not to avoid the product entirely.

## Worked Examples

**Product that passed all 5 filters:**
A posture corrector in the $35-$45 range. Visual payoff: visible transformation when put on. Impulse price: yes. Margin: 15% commission on $40 = $6/sale, break-even at 2 sales. Demand: 50+ videos with 100K+ views, 2,000+ Amazon reviews. Saturation: 3 AI skeleton videos in recent results. This product is worth filming.

**Product that failed Filter 1:**
A magnesium supplement for sleep. Price is right ($28). Margin is decent. Demand is high. But there's no visual payoff : you can't show someone's sleep improving in a 30-second video. Failed Filter 1 immediately. Move on.

**Product that failed Filter 5:**
A LED strip light kit. Strong visual payoff. Good price. Strong demand. But searching TikTok showed 30+ recent AI skeleton videos for this exact product. Saturation is too high to compete effectively without a very different angle. Passed 4 filters, failed 1. Worth considering only if you can find a significantly differentiated hook.

## How to Find Products to Run Through the Filters

1. TikTok Shop Creator Center : the "Find Products" tab shows trending items and available commissions
2. TikTok's trending page : products appearing organically in trending content are already demand-proven
3. Amazon Movers and Shakers : products gaining rapid traction that haven't been picked up by TikTok yet
4. AliExpress trending : earlier stage products that haven't reached TikTok Shop saturation

Spend 30 minutes per week running new products through the filters. Build a shortlist. Film from the shortlist.

Product selection is the decision that determines your ceiling before you've picked up a camera (or opened MidJourney). Get this right and the production work has a return. Get it wrong and the production work is a sunk cost.
